# Above The Rest Painters

Official website for **Above The Rest Painters, Inc.**, family-owned residential and commercial painting contractors serving Tampa Bay, Florida since 1994.

## Pages & Structure

- `index.html` - Homepage with hero painter mascot, services, process, gallery, Before & After interactive slider, reviews, and free estimate form.
- `about.html` - Company story, owner guarantee, licensing, and credentials.
- `services.html` - Overview directory of all services.
- `services-interior.html` - Residential Interior Painting.
- `services-exterior.html` - Residential Exterior Painting & Florida weatherproofing.
- `services-commercial.html` - Commercial Painting for retail, offices, and warehouses.
- `services-pressure-washing.html` - Pressure Washing & Soft Wash cleaning.
- `services-paver-sealing.html` - Brick Paver Cleaning & Sealing.
- `services-stucco-repair.html` - Stucco & Elastomeric Crack Repair.
- `service-areas.html` - County & city breakdown across Greater Tampa Bay.
- `service-areas-tampa.html` - Dedicated Tampa painting contractors page.
- `photos.html` - Filterable photo gallery with category filters.
- `testimonials.html` - Customer reviews and verified awards.
- `contact.html` - Contact page with online quote request and FAQ accordion.
- `blog.html` - Maintenance guides and Florida painting advice.

## Tech Stack & Architecture

- Native HTML5, CSS3 with responsive CSS custom properties, and vanilla ES6+ JavaScript.
- Mobile, tablet, laptop, and desktop responsive layouts.
- Zero client-side bloat, instant page loads, and accessible navigation.
- Configured for zero-config static hosting on **Vercel** with clean URLs enabled via `vercel.json`.
