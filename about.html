Standing "Above" The Rest

Old-Fashioned Service & Quality House Painters

Above The Rest Painters is a licensed, bonded, and insured painting company offering residential and commercial painting, pressure washing, and surface restoration to customers across Hillsborough, Pasco, Pinellas, and Hernando counties.

What stands out most to clients who have used other painting contractors is our personal accountability. We never sub-contract our work to third parties. Every job is handled by our experienced crew, and owner Fred Ferrer oversees each site personally.

Our business has earned its longevity through consistent work and a core belief that retaining old customs still works: a handshake that means our word, a clean job site at the end of each day, and payment only when you are 100% satisfied.
