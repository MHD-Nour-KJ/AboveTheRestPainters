Frequently Asked Questions

Answers To Common Questions

Do you require an upfront deposit before starting work?
No! We never require upfront deposits for our residential painting projects. You only settle payment when the job is completely finished, cleaned, and approved during our final walkthrough.
What brand of paint do you use?
We primarily use top-tier Sherwin-Williams paints (such as SuperPaint and Duration), as well as Benjamin Moore and Behr premium coatings engineered specifically for Florida's intense sunlight and humidity.
Do you sub-contract your painting jobs?
Never. We do not sub-contract work to outside crews. Our in-house painters handle every project, and company owner Fred Ferrer oversees every site personally to ensure the highest standards.
Is pressure washing included with exterior painting?
Yes! Full pressure washing is included completely free with our exterior painting services to ensure all mold, dirt, and loose oxidized paint are removed before primer and paint application.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards including Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover, as well as checks and cash upon completion.
